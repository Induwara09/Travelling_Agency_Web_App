# Optional Vecteezy image replacement guide

The bundled website does **not** redistribute Vecteezy photographs. Vecteezy
downloads are licensed to the downloading user and free assets normally require
attribution. Before replacing an image:

1. Sign in to your own Vecteezy account.
2. Open the asset page and confirm the current licence shown for that asset.
3. Download it yourself and keep the licence/download record.
4. Add the creator/page attribution exactly as Vecteezy provides it.
5. Optimise the file to WebP, copy it into `src/assets/`, then update
   `src/data/siteData.js`.

Useful starting points requested for this project:

- Sri Lanka photo search: https://www.vecteezy.com/free-photos/sri-lanka
- Bentota beach: https://www.vecteezy.com/photo/3646457-beautiful-beach-with-palms-in-bentota-sri-lanka
- Sigiriya search/result page: https://www.vecteezy.com/free-photos/sigiriya
- Sri Lanka tea plantation search: https://www.vecteezy.com/free-photos/tea-plantation-sri-lanka
- Sri Lanka wildlife search: https://www.vecteezy.com/free-photos/sri-lanka-wildlife

Current agreement: https://www.vecteezy.com/licensing-agreement

Do not remove a required credit and do not hotlink a preview image. The original
bundled images are the safe defaults until the project owner completes this
licensing step.
